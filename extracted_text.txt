1.  Purpose, Scope & Objectives
Purpose
This BSAR defines the functional and technical requirements for LogiSync, a single platform that runs the complete operations of a small or mid-size Indian fleet owner (10–50 trucks) across both Full-Truck-Load (FTL) and Part-Truck-Load (PTL) business — from booking through delivery, profitability and settlement. It is written as a build specification: it enumerates personas, the data model, functional modules with traceable requirement IDs, end-to-end workflows, role permissions, integrations, non-functional requirements and a phased release plan, with annotated UI wireframes.
Why FTL and PTL in one system
FTL is trip-centric: one contract maps to one truck, one trip and one profit-and-loss line. PTL is consignment-centric: a single vehicle carries many consignments (LRs) from different shippers, consolidated at hubs, billed on different terms and delivered on separate runs. LogiSync unifies both by making the Consignment (LR) and the Trip first-class records that share one fleet, one driver app, one ledger and one client portal. PTL simply adds a Hub/Manifest consolidation layer between booking and dispatch; everything downstream (tracking, ledger, billing) is common.
In-scope objectives
Single source of truth for fleet, drivers, parties, agents and hired-vehicle owners.
Unified booking for FTL trips and PTL consignments with an auto-rate engine.
Multi-branch hierarchy: Head Office to Branch to Hub to Warehouse, with branch-scoped roles and roll-up P&L.
Hub stock, manifest consolidation and transshipment (crossing) for PTL line-haul.
Warehouse inbound receipt (GRN), per-article barcode/QR material tagging, putaway, stock and outbound pick.
Order-to-cash and statutory compliance: quotation/order, GST/RCM/TDS billing with e-invoice, and physical-POD-driven collection.
Field-cash control: driver trip advances, branch collection and remittance to Head Office.
Offline-first driver app with OTP login, odometer/photo capture, OCR expense capture and ePOD.
Automatic Digital Ledger computing net profit at LR, trip, branch and company level.
Dual-side billing & settlement: client invoicing/To-Pay, agent commissions, lorry-owner hire.
Client portal with quote intake, live ETA and document repository, plus WhatsApp/SMS automation.
Out of scope (this version)
Marketplace/load-exchange matching, GST return filing and full general-ledger accounting (we generate GST invoices and e-invoices but export ledgers to Tally/GST tools), and driver payroll. Warehousing, statutory billing and order-to-cash are in scope (Modules M5 and M14–M17). These remain candidates for later phases or third-party integration.

Key relationships
FTL: Trip 1—1 LR. PTL: Manifest 1—* LR; an LR can traverse multiple manifest legs via crossing (LR—* ManifestLeg).
Trip/Manifest —* Expense; Expenses aggregate into one or more Ledger entries.
LR/Trip —* Document (e-way bill, invoice, POD); documents are client-portal visible when scoped to the party.
Agent/VehicleOwner/Party each own a Ledger; settlement nets debits and credits per cycle.
Branch hierarchy: Head Office 1-* Branch; a Branch may act as a Hub and/or own Warehouse(s); Warehouse 1-* Storage Bin.
Warehousing: GRN 1-* Material Tag; each tag binds an article to its LR and private mark and is scanned at putaway, manifest, crossing and delivery.
Order-to-cash: Quotation 1-* Order/Indent; an Order spawns a Trip or LRs; delivery + physical POD triggers Invoice, then Collection and Settlement.
Cash: a Driver Advance reconciles against trip Expenses; branch To-Pay collection rolls up to a Cash Remittance to Head Office.
Compliance: an Invoice carries GST/RCM and TDS and an e-invoice IRN; the e-way bill links to the LR/Trip.

6.  End-to-End Operational Workflows
6.1  FTL trip lifecycle
Created → Assigned (truck+driver) → Started (odometer+photo) → In-transit (GPS, FASTag, expense capture) → Delivered (POD) → Closed (auto P&L) → Invoiced → Settled. Automations: WhatsApp 'Dispatched' on start; '10 km' on geofence; 'POD uploaded' on delivery; FASTag tolls stream into the ledger throughout.
6.2  PTL consignment lifecycle
Booked (LR at origin branch) → Inbound (GRN + per-article barcode/QR tag + putaway to bin) → At Hub → Manifested (picked from bins, consolidated to a vehicle for a lane) → In-transit → [Crossing → re-scan → Manifested]* (zero or more transshipments) → Outbound pick (scan-out) → Out for Delivery (DRS) → Delivered (per-LR ePOD) → Billed. Shared line-haul costs are allocated to each LR by chargeable weight when the leg closes.
6.3  Hired-vehicle (broker) settlement flow
Lorry Hire Note created with agreed hire (bhada) → Advance paid at loading → vehicle runs the trip/manifest → Balance becomes due on POD, net of deductions (shortages, late delivery, mamool as misc) → Settled, posted to the vehicle-owner ledger. Booking/delivery-agent commissions settle on a parallel cycle against amounts they collected for the HO.
6.4  Warehousing inbound / outbound (material tagging)
Inbound: a GRN is raised against the LR; each article is tagged with a barcode/QR bound to the LR and its private mark, then put away to a storage bin. Outbound: a pick list is generated from the manifest and tags are scanned out and loaded. The same tag is scanned at putaway, manifest, crossing and delivery — giving full chain of custody and automatic shortage detection. FTL goods received into a godown before loading use the same inbound tagging.
6.5  Quotation to order to vehicle sourcing
Enquiry -> Quotation (rate, validity) -> customer confirms -> Order/Indent -> vehicle sourcing (own truck assigned, or a placement indent raised to a broker for a market lorry) -> vehicle reported/placed -> Booking (a Trip for FTL, or LRs for PTL). GSTIN and vehicle (VAHAN) / driver (Sarathi) details are validated during sourcing.
6.6  Order-to-cash: physical POD to collection
On delivery the ePOD is captured and the physical signed POD travels back to the branch. Receipt of the physical POD triggers the GST invoice (forward-charge or RCM as applicable, with an e-invoice IRN), To-Pay collection at the delivery branch, and TDS reconciliation when the customer deducts 194C. Collections are remitted from the branch to Head Office.
6.7  Driver advance (imprest) reconciliation
A trip advance is issued to the driver; fuel, toll and misc expenses captured in the app draw down against it; on trip close the advance is reconciled to actuals and the balance recovered or topped up, posting to the driver ledger.
6.8  Claims & returns
Shortage or damage noted at delivery raises a claim against the LR (quantity/value), routed for assessment and deduction/settlement or an insurance claim. Undelivered consignments follow a Return-to-Origin path with rebooking; detention and demurrage are captured as accessorial charges.

9.  Non-Functional Requirements
Offline-first - driver app must fully function in shadow zones; queued writes sync with deterministic conflict resolution (last-writer-wins per field + server reconciliation for ledger).
Performance - dashboard and live map refresh within ~2-3 s for up to ~50 vehicles; ledger recompute on trip close is near-instant.
Security & privacy - OTP auth, role/branch RBAC, encryption in transit and at rest, Aadhaar masking, signed URLs for documents.
Scalability - multi-branch, multi-tenant ready; horizontal scale of API and notification workers.
Reliability - notification and FASTag/OCR jobs are retried idempotently; no double-posting to the ledger.
Localization - Hindi + key regional languages in the driver app; Indian number/Rs formatting; low-bandwidth assets.
Auditability - immutable audit log for rate overrides, settlements, deletions and permission changes.

11.  Glossary of Trade Terms
Term
Meaning
LR / GR / Bilty / CN
Lorry Receipt / Goods Receipt / Consignment Note - the core legal receipt for booked goods.
Challan
Generic note: booking/loading challan, delivery challan, or truck challan (lorry hire note).
Private mark
Shipper's identifying mark on each package for hub sorting and delivery matching without opening.
GRN
Goods Receipt Note - records articles received into a warehouse against an LR.
Material tag
Per-article barcode/QR bound to the LR and private mark; scanned through the chain of custody.
Putaway / Bin
Placing tagged goods into a storage bin/rack location in the warehouse.
Godown
Warehouse / storage shed at a branch or hub.
Articles
Count of physical packages in a consignment.
Chargeable weight
Higher of actual and volumetric weight; basis for PTL freight.
To-Pay / Paid / TBB
Freight terms: paid by consignee on delivery / prepaid / to-be-billed on monthly credit.
BOD
Basis of Delivery - customer pays after partial delivery before the balance is released.
Hamali
Loading/unloading labour charge, usually recovered from the customer.
Bhada / Hire
Freight paid to a hired (attached/market) lorry owner.
Advance & Balance
Lorry owner paid an advance at loading and balance at unloading against POD.
Manifest / Trip sheet
Document tying many LRs to one vehicle for a line-haul leg.
Crossing / Transshipment
Moving consignments between vehicles at a hub en route.
DRS
Delivery Run Sheet - destination delivery list for a vehicle/area.
ePOD
Electronic Proof of Delivery (photo + signature).
Mamool
Customary informal/incidental payments en route; tracked as a misc expense.
Kanta / Dharam kanta
Weighbridge; source of chargeable weight.
Detention / Halting
Charges for vehicle/goods held beyond free time.
Quotation / Indent
Priced offer to a customer; the indent/order is the confirmed load request that follows.
GTA / RCM
Goods Transport Agency; under GST, freight is often taxed by reverse charge (the recipient pays).
TDS (194C)
Tax deducted at source by customers on freight payments; reconciled against receipts.
e-Invoice / IRN
GST e-invoice with an Invoice Reference Number from the IRP, required for B2B above threshold.
Imprest / Trip advance
Cash advanced to a driver for trip expenses, reconciled against actuals.
RTO (Return to Origin)
An undelivered consignment returned to the booking point for rebooking.
VAHAN / Sarathi
Government portals/APIs for vehicle (RC) and driving-licence verification.
